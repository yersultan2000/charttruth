    The AI reads. The math decides.
    Asking a chatbot “is this misleading?” gives an opinion you can’t check. ChartTruth splits the job so every verdict traces back to numbers.
    
    
      
      
    1. AI reads
      Gemini or Claude turns the image into numbers and visual cues. Cross-check mode uses both and compares them.
      2. 
      
    2. You check
      Every number is editable, with undo. When the AI is unsure or the two models disagree, ChartTruth waits for you.
      3. 
      
    3. Rules decide
      Exaggeration (lie factor), tick spacing, slice sums, flipped axes, two axes and 3D, plus a guardrail for honest zoomed charts.
      4. 
      
    4. Honest redraw
      Zero baseline, even ticks, upright, flat, pies rescaled to 100%, with a caption and alt text.
      5. 
    
    
    
    Asking an AI directly
    • Answer can change between runs
    • Can’t show its working
    • Often flags any axis not at zero
    • Can’t be corrected
    
      
    ChartTruth
    • Same numbers, same verdict
    • Every flag shows its math
    • Guardrail protects honest science charts
    • Fix a number and the verdict updates

    Our rules for ourselves
    
    
    
      
    No guessing
    Below 60% confidence, or when two models disagree, it says “can’t verify yet”.
    
      
    No false alarms
    Zoomed science and finance line charts get a neutral note, not a misleading flag.
    
      
    Design, not people
    A flag describes the chart’s design. It never claims anyone meant to deceive.
    
      
    You’re in control
    Every value is editable, with undo, and you can mark the data as checked.
    
      
    Private
    No servers, no tracking. Images go to an AI only with your own key.
    
      
    Accessible
    Alt text for every correction, keyboard support, reduced motion.
    
      
    Honest limits
    One data series at a time. It can’t catch cherry-picked dates, bad captions or bad source data.
    
      
    Open method
    The lie factor follows Edward Tufte’s idea. Rules, prompts and benchmark code are public.

    How to use ChartTruth
    
  
    1. Pick a chart. Upload or paste an image, or click an example.
    2. Read the numbers. Let Gemini or Claude read them, or type them yourself.
    3. See what’s wrong. The verdict explains the problem. Drag the circle to compare the published and honest charts.
    4. Share. Copy the honest caption, download the image, or copy a link.
