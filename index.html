    The AI reads. The math decides.
    Asking a chatbot “is this misleading?” gives an opinion you can’t check. I split the job so every verdict traces back to numbers.

      AI reads

    4. Rules
      Honest redraw

    Asking an AI directly
    • Answer can change between runs
    • Can’t show its working
    • Often flags any axis not at zero
    • Can’t be corrected
    
      
    ChartTruth
    • Same numbers, same verdict
    • Every flag shows its math
    • Protects honest science charts
    • Fix a number and the verdict updates

    My rules for myself
    
    
    
      
    No guessing
    If the AI is unsure or two AIs disagree, I say “can’t verify yet”.
    
      
    No false alarms
    Zoomed science and finance line charts get a gentle note, not a red flag.
    
      
    Design, not people
    A flag is about the chart’s design. I never say anyone meant to lie.
    
      
    You’re the boss
    Every value is editable, with undo, and you can mark it as checked.
    
      
    Private
    No servers, no tracking. Images go to an AI only with your own key.
    
      
    For everyone
    Alt text for every fix, keyboard support, reduced motion.
    
      
    Honest limits
    One data series at a time. I can’t catch cherry-picked dates or bad source data.
    
      
    Open method
    The lie factor follows Edward Tufte’s idea. All my rules and code are public.
